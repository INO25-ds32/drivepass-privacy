DrivePass keeps a road trip on your iPhone. There is no account. Do not operate the app while driving.

Support

A trip needs Location When In Use. Choose Always if you want the trail to continue with the screen off. Location updates stop when you end the trip.

To share a car with passengers, they scan the driver’s QR code or enter the 4-character cabin code.

Export a zip backup from My Page. Banner ads may appear when stopped; they are hidden during the driving lock. You can turn banners off in Settings.

Contact

Use App Store product-page feedback or review replies. There is no account, so we cannot see or change data on your device.
