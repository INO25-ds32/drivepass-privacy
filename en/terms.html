DrivePass Terms of Use

Last updated: 14 October 2026

DrivePass is for personal trip records. It is not a car navigation or driver-assistance system.

Safety

Do not operate this app while driving. Photos, talk prompts, search, ratings, and route checks are for after you stop, or for a passenger. The driving lock is only an aid. Traffic law and safety always come first. The developer is not liable for harm from distracted driving.

In-app routes are a reference for a stopped car or a passenger. Do not rely on turn-by-turn voice guidance while driving.

Accuracy

Location, distance, weather, place search, and routes are estimates and may be missing without a network or permission.

Passby

Content sent nearby or by QR stays on the other device. This is not a public social network. Delete received cards you do not want. Do not send inappropriate content.

Disclaimer

The app is provided as is. To the extent allowed by law, the developer is not liable for lost data, outages of weather, maps, ads, or lyrics lookup, or device failure. Export a zip backup from My Page.

Ads

Banner ads may appear on some screens when stopped. They are hidden during the driving lock and when kid bingo is on. You can turn them off in Settings.
