DrivePass Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

DrivePass records road trips on your device. We do not create accounts, we do not include an analytics SDK, and we do not track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

1. What stays on the device

Trip trails, stops, photos, short voice notes, journals, car notes, emergency contacts, and packing lists are stored on your iPhone (and in the App Group used by widgets if you add them). The app has no upload-to-our-server feature.

2. What may leave the device

  • Weather: coordinates at a stop are sent to Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com) to label the sky. No account.
  • Nearby places: “Need now” uses Apple MapKit search, under Apple’s policies.
  • Maps and routes: your current location may be sent to Apple’s directions services for a reference route (car, walk, transit). This is for use when stopped or by a passenger, not as in-car turn-by-turn navigation.
  • Maps app: opening a pin may pass coordinates to Apple Maps or another maps app on the device.
  • Passby / QR: while the app is open, nearby DrivePass devices may exchange a trip summary and optional stop photos. Same-car cabin codes and vehicles still moving are excluded. You can pause this on the Passby tab. There is no public feed.
  • Export: zip backups, vlogs, and saving to Photos happen only when you tap.
  • Ads: Google AdMob banners may appear on My Page, trip recap, and Passby when the car is not moving. They are hidden during the driving lock and when kid bingo is on. Trip location is not used for ads. We do not show an App Tracking Transparency prompt. You can hide banners in Settings.
  • Now playing: for in-cabin lyrics we read the system now-playing title, artist, and playback position. If the device has no lyrics, title, artist, and duration are sent to LRCLIB (lrclib.net) to look up public lyrics. Location and accounts are not sent.

3. Location

During an active trip we use location for the trail and stop detection. Always authorization lets tracking continue with the screen off, only for that trip. Updates stop when the trip ends. This is not a dashcam. Location is not used for ad targeting.

4. Camera, microphone, photos

Used after you stop, for a photo or a short (about 10 second) voice note. Photo Library add access is used only if you save an export.

5. Bluetooth and local network

Used while the app is open to find nearby devices. You can pause this on the Passby tab.

6. Notifications

Local reminders you allow, such as rest hints.

7. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. Use with a parent or guardian.

8. Access and deletion

Data lives on the device. Delete the app, export or restore a zip from My Page, or delete received cards. We cannot access personal data remotely because there is no account.
